Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

VoIP Made Simple Inc. ("voIPmsi," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through voipmsi.com (the "Site") and when you contact us for a quote or service, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site or submitting information to us, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us. When you fill out our quote request form, contact form, call us, or email us, we may collect your name, business name, business address or city, phone number, email address, the service you're interested in, and any other details you choose to share.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing activity, including your IP address, browser type, referring/exit pages, and the pages you view, using cookies, log files, and similar technologies, including visitor-identification technology that can associate a website visit with a business or contact record before you submit a form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to quote requests and inquiries, and provide estimates for our services (business internet and ISP selection, network setup, structured cabling, business VoIP phone systems, security cameras, and office moves/telecom buildouts);
  • Communicate with you about your account, request, or an active project;
  • Send you marketing and promotional messages about our services, where permitted, and where you have not opted out;
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site and our services;
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tools (including third-party analytics and visitor-identification services) to understand how visitors use the Site and to support our sales and marketing process. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect how parts of the Site function.

4. Calls, Texts, and Marketing Communications

If you provide your phone number, you agree that VoIP Made Simple Inc. may contact you by phone call or text message (SMS), including using automated dialing or messaging technology, at the number provided, to respond to your request or to share information about our products and services. Consent to receive calls or texts is not a condition of purchasing any service.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying "STOP," or ask to be placed on our do-not-call list by contacting us using the information in Section 10 below.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who perform functions on our behalf, such as our customer relationship management (CRM) platform, hosting provider, email/SMS delivery, and scheduling tools;
  • Manufacturer and technology partners (for example, Grandstream, Yealink, Cisco, or RingCentral) only as needed to fulfill, license, or support equipment and services you purchase;
  • Professional advisors, or authorities, where required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect our rights and safety;
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We will not share your opt-in to an SMS campaign with any third party for purposes unrelated to providing you with the services of that campaign. We may share your Personal Data, including your SMS opt-in or consent status, with third parties that help us provide our messaging services, including but not limited to platform providers, phone companies, and any other vendors who assist us in the delivery of text messages. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including to maintain business records, service active accounts, and comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, opt out of marketing calls, texts, or emails, or withdraw previously given consent, by contacting us using the information in Section 10. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable state privacy laws; we will honor valid requests as required by law.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for businesses and adults, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Third-Party Links

The Site may link to third-party websites we do not control. This Policy does not apply to those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above reflects the most recent revision. Your continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
