Structured Cabling: Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer a free cabling site survey?

Yes. Before we quote anything, we walk your space, measure the runs, count the drops and identify obstacles, so there are no surprises on installation day. The site survey is free and there's no obligation.

What areas do you serve for structured cabling?

We're based in Lauderhill and install structured cabling throughout Broward County: Fort Lauderdale, Plantation, Sunrise, Tamarac, Davie, Coral Springs, Oakland Park and beyond. Being local means we're on-site fast and we know the buildings.

Cat6 or Cat6a: which do we need?

It depends on your bandwidth needs and how long you want the cabling to last. Cat6 handles gigabit easily for most offices; Cat6a supports 10-gigabit over longer runs and is a smart future-proofing choice for new build-outs. We'll recommend based on your actual usage and budget.

Will the work be tested and documented?

Always. Every drop is tested and certified, labeled at both ends, and delivered with as-built documentation and a test report. The next technician who touches your network, including us, will know exactly what's where.
