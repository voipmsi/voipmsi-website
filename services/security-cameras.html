Business Security Cameras: Frequently Asked Questions

Are these business cameras or the kind I'd buy at a big-box store?

Business-grade. We install professional IP camera systems with ruggedized hardware, encrypted streams, proper retention storage and clean, labeled cabling, built for compliance, HR and insurance needs, not a consumer doorbell.

Can I view my cameras from my phone?

Yes. View any camera live from your phone, tablet or laptop, review recorded footage, and receive motion or AI-based alerts, from anywhere and on any device.

How long is footage stored?

It depends on your camera count, resolution and retention needs. We size NVR storage accordingly and can add hybrid cloud backup so footage survives even if on-site hardware is damaged or stolen. We'll recommend a retention plan that fits your requirements.

Do you run the cabling for the cameras too?

We do. We install the low-voltage cabling and PoE infrastructure with the same clean, labeled standard as the rest of your network, ideally during a build-out while walls and pathways are open.
