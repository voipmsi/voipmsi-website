Hosted, On-Premise, or Hybrid?

There's no single “best” VoIP system, only the best fit for your business. Here's an honest comparison of all three.

Hosted Cloud VoIP

Your phone system lives in the cloud. No PBX hardware on site.

Advantages

  • No upfront hardware investment
  • Scales up or down in minutes
  • Works from office, home and mobile
  • Carrier-grade redundancy included
  • Predictable monthly per-seat cost

Trade-offs

  • Ongoing monthly per-seat fees
  • Depends on reliable internet (we design for this)

Best for

Remote/hybrid teams, multi-site businesses, and offices that want enterprise features without capital outlay.

On-Premise IP PBX

You own the PBX. It runs on site for full control.

Advantages

  • Full system ownership, with no per-seat fees
  • Deep customization and integrations
  • Great for compliance-sensitive offices
  • Lower long-term cost at scale

Trade-offs

  • Higher upfront hardware cost
  • You own maintenance (we support it)

Best for

Businesses with strong internal IT, compliance needs, existing infrastructure, or many seats where owning pays off.

Hybrid VoIP

On-premise control where you need it, cloud flexibility where you don't.

Advantages

  • Keep on-site control + cloud mobility
  • Smooth path from legacy to cloud
  • Per-location flexibility
  • Resilience across sites

Trade-offs

  • More design up front (we handle it)
  • Requires a partner who knows both worlds

Best for

Multi-location operations and businesses transitioning from a legacy system at their own pace.

Business VoIP: Frequently Asked Questions

Hosted, on-premise or hybrid: which is right for us?

It depends on your team size, how mobile your staff is, your budget structure (monthly vs. upfront), and any compliance needs. Hosted suits most small and multi-site businesses; on-premise pays off at scale or where control matters; hybrid bridges the two. We assess your situation and recommend honestly, and we install all three.

Can we keep our existing phone numbers?

Yes. We handle number porting from your current carrier so you keep every number. We schedule the port carefully to avoid any gap in service.

Do you install and support the system locally?

Yes. We're based in Lauderhill and provide local installation, configuration, staff training and ongoing support, not a remote 1-800 line. We program your auto attendants, call queues and voicemail-to-email and make sure it all works.

Will our internet handle VoIP calls?

That's exactly why we also design networks. We size your connection and tune your network for clear, reliable calls, adding voice VLAN, QoS and PoE switching where needed. Poor call quality is almost always a network issue, and we fix it at the source.
