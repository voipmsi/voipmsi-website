A Move Is the Worst Time for a Surprise

An office move touches everything at once: the internet has to be ordered weeks ahead, the cabling has to go in before the furniture, the network and phones have to be live the moment you walk in, and your numbers have to follow you. Miss one dependency and opening day becomes a scramble. We coordinate the whole sequence so it doesn't.

Office Moves: Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we contact you about a move?

As early as possible, ideally 6 to 8 weeks out. Business internet is the long pole: ordering and installing a new circuit can take weeks, and everything else (cabling, network, phones) sequences around it. The earlier we start, the smoother the cutover.

Can you keep our phone numbers when we move?

Yes. We port your existing numbers to your new system and schedule the port around your cutover so there's no gap in service. Customers keep reaching you on the same numbers.

Will there be downtime during the move?

Our goal is zero downtime. We get the new location fully built and tested before cutover, then sequence the switch so the new office is live before the old one goes dark. A clear cutover plan is the difference between a smooth move and a chaotic one.

Do we have to manage all the different vendors?

No, that's the point. Instead of juggling an internet company, a cabling crew, a network person and a phone vendor, you work with one local team that coordinates all of it and owns the result.
