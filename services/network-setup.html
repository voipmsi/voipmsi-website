Network Setup: Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my network matter for my phone system?

VoIP runs over your data network. Mis-configured switches, no QoS, an overloaded firewall or weak Wi-Fi are the most common causes of dropped and choppy calls. We design the network and the phone system together, so voice is prioritized and reliable from day one.

Can you fix or improve our existing network?

Yes. A large part of our work is troubleshooting and upgrading networks installed by someone else, replacing consumer gear with business-grade equipment, adding VLANs and QoS, fixing Wi-Fi dead zones, and documenting what's actually in the rack.

Do you handle the cabling too?

We do. Network equipment is only as good as the cabling behind it. We install and certify the Cat6/Cat6a and fiber, terminate the patch panels, and mount the racks. See our structured cabling page for details.

Do you offer business Wi-Fi installation in Broward County?

Yes. Business Wi-Fi installation across Broward County is one of our core services. We survey your space, plan access-point placement for full coverage, run the cabling, and configure secure staff and guest networks.
